# 홍보 이미지 컨셉 10선 (생성형 AI 프롬프트)

> 사장님 제공(2026-10-21). `ek-design`은 세트마다 이 중 **한 가지 컨셉만** 골라 그 세트 전체(표지~마지막 장)를 같은 세계관으로 만든다.
> 생성 이미지 규칙: 이미지 안에 **글자·로고·포장 브랜드·가짜 앱 화면을 넣지 않는다**(프롬프트 끝에 `No text, no letters, no logos, no packaging, no brand names` 추가). 문구는 HTML로 얹고, 앱 화면은 실제 스크린샷을 폰 프레임에 합성한다.
> 기능 주장은 실제 앱에 있는 것만 — 아래 활용 팁 중 확인 안 된 기능(예: 3번 다매장 그래프, 7번 무선 라벨 프린터 연결)은 문구로 쓰지 않는다. 7번은 "이미지로 저장해서 인쇄"까지만.

## 프롬프트 원문

1. **매직 바코드 스캐너** — A 3D illustration of a modern smartphone scanning a barcode on a snack packaging. Floating holographic calendar icons and "D-Day" text are popping out of the screen dynamically. Vibrant colors, clean light blue background, modern UI mockup style, 8k resolution, highly detailed.
2. **평화로운 주방** — Top-down view of a pristine, organized kitchen counter bathed in soft morning sunlight. A sleek smartphone in the center displays a green checkmark. Surrounding the phone are fresh, vibrant vegetables, tofu, and milk. Photorealistic, warm lifestyle photography, commercial advertisement style.
3. **스마트 편의점 통합 관리** — Isometric 3D rendering of a neat mini convenience store. Glowing digital price tags and green checkmarks are floating above the snack shelves indicating product freshness. In the foreground, a tablet acts as a smart control hub displaying graphs. Cute claymation style, pastel colors, soft lighting, clean and professional.
4. **수입 과자 날짜 해독기** — Close-up macro shot of a smartphone camera scanning an imported Chinese snack with complex foreign text. A bright glowing laser beam from the phone is magically translating the text into a clear, glowing digital calendar icon "2026-10-15". Sci-fi elements, cinematic depth of field, dark background with neon highlights.
5. **식재료 심폐소생술** — A conceptual split-screen image. On the left side, raw ingredients like tofu, green onions, and kimchi are glowing slightly. On the right side, a steaming, delicious bowl of Korean Kimchi stew. A modern smartphone is in the middle bridging the two sides. Cinematic lighting, professional food photography, appetizing.
6. **폐기율 0% 철벽 방어** — Conceptual minimalist art. A glowing smartphone acting as a magical energy shield, protecting fresh colorful groceries from falling into a dark trash bin below. Dark background, dramatic lighting, high contrast, visually striking, conveying safety and savings.
7. **스마트 라벨 프린터** — A modern smartphone wirelessly connected to a sleek mini thermal printer on a wooden retail counter. Crisp, clean price tags with barcodes are being printed out. Bright daylight, hyper-realistic, shallow depth of field, commercial product photography.
8. **온 가족 냉장고 동기화** — 2D flat vector illustration of a happy family standing together. Each family member is looking at their own smartphone, and all phones show the exact same colorful digital fridge inventory with sync icons. Warm orange and yellow color palette, friendly corporate tech illustration style, clean and modern.
9. **여유로운 알림** — A relaxing living room scene. In the blurred background, a person is comfortably reading a book on the couch. In sharp focus in the foreground, a smartphone rests on a coffee table, displaying a cute animated bell icon with a "D-3" alert notification. Warm ambient lighting, cozy and stress-free atmosphere.
10. **직관적인 달력 뷰** — A 3D illustration of a large, beautiful digital calendar floating above a smartphone. Certain dates on the calendar are glowing with small red and green tags representing food items. Clean, smooth 3D render, soft pastel colors, modern UI/UX design, highly detailed, visually satisfying.
